One club. Every campus in the city.

Cardinals brings students from different schools and programs into one inclusive tech community. We learn cloud and technology together, build real projects, and connect with people already working in the industry.

The club runs on a centralized executive structure: an Executive Leadership, five offices, and a Creatives and Multimedia division. Every officer has a clear role and a clear way to contribute.

  • LearningWorkshops, study sessions and certification paths for every skill level.
  • CommunityAn inclusive space where students from every school collaborate.
  • IndustrySpeakers, partners and sponsors who open doors beyond campus.
  • ProjectsHands-on builds, events and campaigns with real responsibility.
